Privacy Policy — BoomLine

Effective date: September 14, 2026 Last updated: September 14, 2026

BoomLine ("the Game") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

App BoomLine

2. Information We Collect

2-1. Stored on your device only (never transmitted)

The following is stored solely on your device and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the Game.

  • Game progress (stages cleared, stars earned, best scores, attempt counts)
  • In-game currency state (hearts, coins, and refill timestamps)
  • Settings (language, theme, font, sound set, effect options)
  • Nickname you typed and profile image you selected
  • Tutorial completion flags
  • A local play log (written to a file on your device only; never uploaded)

2-2. Services the current version does not use

The current version of the Game includes no third-party services of any kind — no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or account sign-in — and does not transmit any user information over the internet.

We may adopt the services below in the future. If we do, we will update this policy and the Google Play "Data safety" section before releasing that version.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase / Firebase Crashlytics — device information, advertising ID, usage events, crash reports
  • Google Play Games Services (only if you choose to sign in) — account identifier, display name, leaderboard and achievement records
  • Advertising (Google AdMob or similar, only if you choose to watch an ad) — advertising ID, device information, ad impression and click records

The Game shows no forced advertising. Even if ads are introduced, they will play only when you actively choose "watch for a reward."

2-3. What we do not collect

  • Name, date of birth, gender, address, phone number, or other identity data
  • Contacts, photos, precise location, call logs, or files
  • Camera or microphone access

(When you pick a profile image, only that single image is copied into the app's private storage; the Game does not access your photo library as a whole.)

3. How Information Is Used

  • Saving and restoring game progress
  • Keeping your settings
  • (If adopted in the future) diagnosing errors and improving stability, improving content quality such as difficulty tuning, providing leaderboards, and serving rewarded ads

Information is not used for any other purpose, and no analysis is performed to identify individual users.

4. Retention and Deletion

  • Device-stored data is deleted when you uninstall the Game.
  • You can delete your progress yourself in-game via Settings → Reset.
  • (If adopted in the future) data collected by third-party services is retained and deleted under their own policies.

5. Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your information. The current version has no third-party data processors. If the services below are adopted, this table will be updated beforehand.

6. Your Rights

  • Delete game data: In-game, go to Settings → Reset. Uninstalling the Game also removes all device-stored data.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Device Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization."
  • Access, correction, or deletion requests: Contact us at the email above and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe this has occurred may contact us at the email above.

8. Security

  • Device-stored data lives in the app's private storage and is not accessible to other apps.
  • The developer does not hold information that personally identifies you.
  • (If adopted in the future) communication with third-party services uses encrypted (HTTPS) channels.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in-game or in the store release notes.
